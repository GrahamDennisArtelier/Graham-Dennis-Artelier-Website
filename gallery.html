Artist & maker

About Graham Dennis

“I use colour as a place to pause - building and erasing until the surface holds both the history of its making and a space for the viewer.”

01

Artist journey

Graham’s practice grew from a lifelong attention to landscape, architecture and the emotional charge of colour. Each body of work is an evolving visual vocabulary.

02

Creative philosophy

The paintings resist quick reading. Layers, marks and quiet interruptions reward slow looking and allow meaning to remain personal.

03

Studio process

Works begin intuitively. Pigment, oil, wax and mineral matter are added, scraped and rebuilt until balance arrives.

Every artwork is carefully packed and shipped personally by Graham Dennis to ensure it arrives safely and beautifully presented.
