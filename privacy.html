Privacy notice.

This notice explains how Graham Dennis Artelier handles personal information when you browse the website, contact the studio, join the studio list or purchase an artwork.

Last updated 21 August 2026

Who is responsible

Graham Dennis Artelier is the controller of the personal information described here. Contact the studio at studio@grahamdennisartelier.co.uk. The studio’s full geographic trading address must be added here before online orders are accepted.

Information collected

  • Contact and studio-list details, including name, email address, enquiry type and message.
  • Order and delivery details, including purchased artwork, billing or delivery address, payment status and correspondence.
  • Administrative account and security information.
  • Technical information such as browser, device, IP address and service logs generated when the website is used.

Payment-card details are entered into Stripe’s checkout and are not stored by this website.

Why it is used

  • To respond to an enquiry or take steps requested before a contract.
  • To process and deliver an order, perform a contract and provide customer care.
  • To keep legally required business, tax and transaction records.
  • To send studio-list messages where consent has been given. Consent can be withdrawn at any time.
  • To protect the website, prevent fraud and improve administration where the studio has a legitimate interest that does not override individual rights.

Services and recipients

Information is shared only where needed with: Supabase for database, authentication and server functions; Stripe for checkout and payment processing; Cloudflare for website hosting, delivery and security; Zoho for studio email; and relevant delivery carriers, professional advisers or public authorities when required. Each provider handles information under its own terms and safeguards.

If a provider processes information outside the UK, appropriate transfer safeguards relied on by that provider will apply.

How long information is kept

Information is retained only for as long as needed for the purpose collected, including legal, accounting, dispute and fraud-prevention requirements. Studio-list details are held until consent is withdrawn. Enquiries that do not lead to an order should normally be reviewed after 24 months. Order and accounting records may need to be held for up to six years. Provider security logs follow the relevant provider’s retention settings.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or objection, and may withdraw consent. Email the studio to make a request. You may also complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Security and changes

Reasonable technical and organisational safeguards are used, but no internet service is completely secure. This notice may be updated when the website, providers or legal requirements change; the date above will be revised.
