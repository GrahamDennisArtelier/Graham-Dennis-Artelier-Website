This page explains the cookies and similar browser storage technologies used by the website.

Last updated 21 August 2026

Current use

The website does not intentionally use advertising or analytics cookies. It uses essential browser storage to remember the shopping cart and, for authorised studio administrators, to maintain a secure Supabase sign-in session. Cloudflare may use strictly necessary security technology to deliver and protect the site.

Checkout and external services

When you choose to pay, you are redirected to Stripe’s pre-built Checkout. Stripe may set cookies or use similar technology on its own pages for payment, fraud prevention and security. Supabase supports database, authentication and server functions. Cloudflare hosts and protects the site. Zoho processes email after you contact the studio; it does not need to place a cookie on this website for ordinary email delivery.

Your choices

You can remove site data through your browser settings. Doing so may empty the cart or sign an administrator out. Strictly necessary storage does not require consent because the requested service cannot work properly without it.

If optional analytics, marketing, embedded media or other non-essential technology is added later, this notice and the consent controls must be updated before that technology is activated.

Questions

For questions about this notice, email studio@grahamdennisartelier.co.uk. Further information about personal data is in the Privacy Notice.
