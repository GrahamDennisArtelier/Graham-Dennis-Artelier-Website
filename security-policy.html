Responsible disclosure

Security
policy.

We welcome responsible reports that help us protect our visitors, customers and services.

Effective 24 August 2026

Reporting a vulnerability

Email scott@shwdigitalservices.site with a clear description of the issue, the affected URL or component, steps to reproduce it and its potential impact. Please include only the evidence needed to demonstrate the issue.

For sensitive reports, encrypt your message using our public PGP key. Do not include secrets or personal information in an unencrypted message.

In scope

  • The website and services served from grahamdennisartelier.co.uk and www.grahamdennisartelier.co.uk.
  • Vulnerabilities in site functionality that could compromise confidentiality, integrity or availability.
  • Authentication or authorisation flaws, injection, cross-site scripting, exposure of sensitive information and demonstrable security misconfiguration.

Out of scope

  • Third-party services and infrastructure not controlled by Graham Dennis Artelier.
  • Social engineering, phishing, physical attacks or attempts to access staff or customer accounts.
  • Denial-of-service testing, automated high-volume scanning or any action that degrades the service.
  • Reports based only on missing security headers, software version disclosure, self-XSS, clickjacking on pages with no sensitive action, or scanner output without a demonstrable security impact.

Research guidelines

Act in good faith. Use the minimum testing necessary, test only with accounts and data you own or have permission to use, do not access or alter other people’s data, and stop if you encounter personal information or cause disruption. Do not exploit a vulnerability beyond what is required to confirm it, establish persistence, or publicly disclose an unresolved issue.

If you accidentally access sensitive data, stop testing, report it promptly and securely, and delete any retained copies after we confirm receipt.

What you can expect

We will make a reasonable effort to acknowledge your report, investigate it and keep you informed of material progress. Please allow us a reasonable opportunity to resolve a confirmed issue before publishing details. We may recognise helpful researchers on our acknowledgments page with their permission.

Safe harbour

When you follow this policy and act in good faith, we will not pursue legal action against you for your security research. This does not authorise activity against third parties, and we cannot bind independent third parties.
