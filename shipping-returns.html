Last updated 21 August 2026

Delivery

The checkout will request the delivery address and show the total payable before payment through Stripe. Once dispatched, tracking information will be provided where the carrier offers it. International customers should be told before ordering whether import VAT, customs duty or handling fees are included or payable on arrival.

On arrival

Please inspect the package promptly. If an artwork or package arrives damaged, photograph the outer packaging, internal packing and artwork, retain all materials, and email studio@grahamdennisartelier.co.uk as soon as possible. The studio will arrange the appropriate repair, replacement or refund in accordance with your legal rights.

Changing your mind

For eligible standard artwork bought online, UK consumers may notify the studio within 14 days after delivery that they wish to cancel. After notifying the studio, return the work within the following 14 days, unused and in its original condition and protective packaging.

Unless the work is faulty, damaged or misdescribed, the customer normally pays the direct return cost. Because original works can be fragile and valuable, contact the studio before sending anything so a suitable insured service can be agreed.

Refunds and exceptions

Eligible refunds will be made to the original payment method, normally within 14 days after the returned work is received or evidence of return is supplied, subject to the rights permitted by law. The studio may reduce a refund if handling beyond what is reasonably necessary diminishes the artwork’s value.

Commissioned, made-to-order or clearly personalised artwork may not carry a change-of-mind cancellation right. This does not affect rights where goods are faulty, damaged or not as described.
